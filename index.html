Why This Map Matters

Young families face serious challenges in today’s housing market:

  • 71% of younger millennial buyers were first-time homebuyers (National Association of REALTORS®, 2024).
  • 33% received help with their down payment (National Association of REALTORS®, 2024).
  • 43% of millennials are balancing student loan debt with saving for a home (National Association of REALTORS®, 2024).
  • $2,182 is the average monthly cost of full-time childcare for two children (New York Post, 2024).
  • 12% of homebuyers are younger millennials; 3% are Gen Z buyers (National Association of REALTORS®, 2024).
  • Low housing inventory is keeping prices high and limiting options (Amadeo, 2024).

This spatial analysis helps young families explore affordable housing options based on their needs.
